## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`egypt-copy`](skills/egypt-copy/SKILL.md) | Natural Egyptian Arabic product copy: register choice between MSA and dialect, form of address, UI terminology, and error, empty, and pending states. |

Add more under `skills/` in this folder:

```text
countries/egypt/
├── README.md
└── skills/
    └── egypt-copy/
        ├── SKILL.md
        └── references/
```

## Good first skills

- `egypt-product-ux`: phone numbers, addresses, currency display, payment methods, and messaging channels people actually use.
- `egypt-backend`: money, phone, and time-zone data, local providers, and privacy rules.
- `egypt-web-audit`: how to review a site or app for Egypt users.
