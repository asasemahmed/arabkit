## Good first skills

- `comoros-copy`: the product voice for Comoros, when to use the local dialect versus Modern Standard Arabic, and common UI terms.
- `comoros-product-ux`: phone numbers, addresses, currency display, payment methods, and messaging channels people actually use.
- `comoros-backend`: money, phone, and time-zone data, local providers, and privacy rules.
- `comoros-web-audit`: how to review a site or app for Comoros users.
